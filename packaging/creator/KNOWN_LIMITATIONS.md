# Private preview limitations

- Version0.45.0/protocol17 is work in progress. Full Rise of Kingdoms numerical/mechanical parity is not certified; this release changes no troop/AP/Citadel/commander/pass balance.
- This is one local authoritative kingdom on your own PC, not a shared remote server. No firewall/public exposure is enabled. Existing normal saves and active servers are separate.
- Art mixes original procedural structures/creatures, original prototype rigs, AI-generated painted reference art and a more developed Frontier Captain. Many models, surfaces, effects and portraits are placeholders. Eleven commander roster entries do not mean eleven equally finished bespoke production models.
- The offline image/audio index is read-only. Earlier review renders and prepared native scenarios are explicitly labeled. It grants nothing, performs no simulation and is not evidence of organic progression. It is not a new interactive3D gallery.
- All five city cultures, all troop types, all commanders and mythic creatures are not normally unlockable in a10–15-minute fresh-city tour. Training has real timers; advanced models require their usual institutions, technology, recruitment and ownership.
- Seven zones and ten functional passes exist. Fog hides undiscovered terrain/models; public pass labels alone do not reveal defender intelligence. Opening milestones, alliances, connected territory and rallies gate military access/capture. Scouts can cross uncontrolled passes. The owner-approved original Divine Gate encounter is numerically unverified against RoK.
- Prior native QA exercised Greek creation, neutral denial, scouts, actual Eos rally/capture/crossing, bounded camera input and save/restart. Some prerequisites in that separate QA encounter were accelerated with production commands and are not a fresh-player progression certificate. Full individual7-zone/10-model visual acceptance, every map boundary/resolution, middle-button drag and isolated single-border outsider testing remain incomplete.
- Initial city/world camera focus can show unfamiliar unexplored fog; Home [H] is available. This observation has not yet been isolated as a reproducible defect.
- Unity Player logs contain recursive ArmyData serialization-depth warnings. Bounded native restart preserved troops/position/ownership, but that does not prove every serialization path correct.
- Existing short effects are original synthesized placeholders. A finished adaptive soundtrack, faction themes and full creature/voice libraries are proposed production work, not included features. Do not use copied commercial game music/art as replacements.
- The unsigned package is for private collaborators. Use a trusted transfer and compare the ZIP checksum supplied by the owner. Do not disable Windows protections to run it. Preview profiles contain private reconnect data; never share their saves/logs unredacted.
- `Thrones_of_Myth_Creator_Asset_Tracker.xlsx` and `Thrones_of_Myth_Creator_Brief.md` were not available in the supplied files. They are NOT INCLUDED. Attach those exact files to the project owner for the next creator-pack update. The included production brief is explicitly a proposed backlog, not an authoritative gameplay specification or a copy of those documents.
