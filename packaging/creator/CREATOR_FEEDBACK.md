# Private creative tour —10–15 minutes

This is an original work-in-progress art/audio review, not a completed game or numerical parity demonstration. Feedback is welcome on both the existing foundations and proposed replacements. There are no progression grants or hidden showcase gameplay commands.

1. **Minutes0–2: identity.** Extract and launch using START_HERE.txt. Choose a civilization and a new name. Note the entry palette, typography, silhouettes and whether the world feels like restored antiquity rather than a copy of another game.
2. **Minutes2–5: living city.** Inspect the Citadel, Hall of Spears, streets and walls. Orbit Q/E and select several districts. Record whether scale, selection outlines and construction/locked states read clearly. You can view one chosen culture immediately; see the offline civilization contact sheet for the other cultures rather than grinding or changing your save.
3. **Minutes5–8: army and commander.** Return to kingdom, inspect your starting formation, open Commanders and the available model/portrait preview. Compare bronze/cloth, soldier proportions, weapon scale and leader distinction. Not all eleven commanders are owned. Use CREATOR_PACK/index.html for the full painted portrait roster and contact sheets; these are references, not a claim that every portrait is a finished unique3D rig.
4. **Minutes8–11: world.** Send a nearby scout through Marches > Scouting, await the report, then use the minimap, wheel and Home. Inspect an accessible pass label and card. Consider mountain scale, architecture, fog and readable travel routes. A sealed/uncontrolled pass is not a broken free-movement shortcut. All seven zones may not be revealed during this brief tour.
5. **Minutes11–13: combat atmosphere.** If a discovered level1 Forsaken is reachable and your army healthy, inspect and attack normally. Otherwise watch the clearly labeled earlier native combat still in the offline index. Listen for footsteps, impact, selection and cast cues. Art review should not require training a new long-duration batch or attacking a stronger enemy.
6. **Minutes13–15: offline references and audio.** Open CREATOR_PACK/index.html. Inspect culture/unit contact sheets, commander paintings, pass screenshots and original synthesized WAV samples. Mythic escorts need the Sanctuary of Beasts and normal recruitment; the listed five creatures are a production roster, not five instantly playable starters. Full soundtrack cues in the production brief are proposed work, not shipped music.

## Send back

- Your civilization, approximate play time, resolution and ZIP/source version.
- Three things that communicate the world well, and three things that confuse it.
- Screenshots annotated with scale/silhouette/material/readability observations.
- Which one city structure, troop and commander should be replaced first, and why.
- For animation: idle/move/attack/turn/reaction timing and foot sliding; describe the actual clip/state observed.
- For sound: cue name if known, context, loudness, masking, fatigue and spatial clarity. Which moments need music, quiet or transition? Audio listening has not been certified on every device.
- For an apparent gameplay bug: exact clicks, expected/actual result and screenshot. Mark anything you did not reach as NOT TESTED.

Do not send your save, login details or session tokens. The project owner will turn accepted proposals into an agreed asset backlog. The absent owner-supplied tracker/brief are not silently replaced by this proposal.
