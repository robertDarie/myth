# Expanded Athenaeum research

Four technologies join the existing tree. Ranks already researched are unchanged. Each new technology has three ranks. Rank 1 needs Athenaeum 2 and the named prerequisite at rank 1. Rank 3 needs Athenaeum 3. Cost is the base multiplied by the square of the rank being bought. Duration is 12 seconds times that rank, before Runic Scriptoria.

## Drills of the Dawn

Military. Requires Heroic Formations. Base cost: 70 food, 55 gold, 30 stone.

+4% training and promotion speed per rank, up to +12%. This multiplies Hall of Spears speed. An order stores its duration when it is paid, so a later rank does not shorten it.

## Mercy of the Healers

Military. Requires Oaths of Endurance. Base cost: 60 food, 50 gold, 8 Mana.

+6% hospital treatment speed per rank, up to +18%. This multiplies House of Renewal speed. Home recovery is unchanged. A healing job stores its duration when it starts.

## Stores of the Ages

Economy. Requires Blessings of Plenty. Base cost: 50 wood, 45 gold, 25 stone.

+8% passive stockpile per rank, up to +24%, applied to the existing vault and Citadel ceiling and then rounded down. Gathering and battle rewards are not limited by it.

## Caravan Pledges

Economy. Requires Pilgrim Logistics. Base cost: 55 food, 40 gold, 35 wood.

+6% gathering cargo per rank, up to +18%. Mortal troops and mythic escorts both use it. It multiplies the Pilgrim Logistics cargo bonus.

Schema stays 21. A save without these ranks loads them at zero. Protocol stays 16.
