# Forsaken PvE ecosystem

Implementation status: planned after numeric and population checkpoints. Ordinary Forsaken are mortal Lv.1–25, with a shared identity independent of level. Minotaurs and Giants remain civilization recruits, never regular Forsaken.

Forsaken Forts require rallies and a distinct AP/stat/reward table based on fort references. Forsaken War Camps use staged waves with a final mortal commander. Forgotten Keeps use advanced phased encounters. Sanctuary Guardians protect sacred sites and must honor all existing stage and Divine Gate access checks. Godbreaker Champions are rare group bosses; existing Godbreaker strongholds keep their identity and save compatibility. Special encounters require their own measured calibration and must not silently reuse ordinary enemy rewards.

Eclipse Marauders and Eclipse Warcamps are reserved future cross-kingdom event categories. Existing local seasonal objectives do not constitute cross-kingdom matchmaking or KvK. No Eclipse encounter is enabled until a real event lifecycle, participating kingdoms, access rules and safe reward settlement exist.
