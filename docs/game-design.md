# Game direction

## Agreed foundation

**Title:** Thrones of Myth.

The gods have fallen silent. Mortal civilizations build kingdoms, command heroes
and mythical creatures, and compete to restore or claim divine power.

Civilizations draw on existing Greek, Egyptian, Norse, Chinese, and Japanese
traditions. All play on the same map and can join mixed-civilization alliances.
Civilization specialties should change tactics without making any civilization
mandatory for gathering, defense, siege, or alliance participation.

Mortal troops form the army's foundation; mythic units provide specialized powers
and counters. Example Greek creatures include centaurs, minotaurs, cyclopes, and
gorgons. Individual figures such as Medusa can be commanders or bosses.

Commanders have four rarity tiers: green/Common, blue/Rare, purple/Epic, and
gold/Legendary. Epic commanders need lasting roles. The specific roster,
progression, acquisition, and monetization systems are not decided.

## Shared kingdom map

Players begin in outer provinces. Alliances establish citadels and expand
connected territory with flags or beacons. Shrines grant alliance-wide buffs;
passes control access to inner zones. Scheduled openings lead toward a central
sanctuary and kingdom leadership. Civilization choice does not restrict starting
province or alliance membership.

Later kingdom-versus-kingdom campaigns use a separate contested map. Matchmaking
needs to consider active eligible strength rather than raw total power alone.
Detailed rules and seasonal progression remain to be designed.

## PvE

- Forsaken warbands: common roaming mortal enemies.
- Unbound creatures: specialized mythic encounters.
- Ruin strongholds: alliance rally objectives.
- Godbreakers: a potential organized seasonal enemy opposing divine restoration.
- Major mythic bosses: contested encounters deeper in the map.

Hostile allegiance should be explained by circumstance, not creature species.
Neutral enemy variants can occur across the unified map; civilizations do not
own exclusive cultural zones.

## Ideas to validate, not locked requirements

Gods as restoration patrons rather than ordinary army commanders; capturable
divine sites that alter limited map routes; liberation or appeasement of some
creatures. Start by proving movement and authoritative combat before committing
to these larger systems.
