## A — Citadel

Five functional levels, one-to-five deployment slots and age-dependent march bases are real. Base upgrade durations are20/40/60/80 seconds before masonry. The new150-row City Hall comparison contains25 reference levels with six parameters each and explicitly blank unavailable local counterparts. Every available local level2–5 cost/time comparison differs. Twenty reference functional levels are absent. Five original ages can remain thematic categories, but a level mapping requires coordinated costs, prerequisites, research, military capacity and migration decisions. No mapping is approved by this audit.

## B — Every building

The local catalogue has17 definitions including Citadel; builders caps at1 and other buildings at5. The shared mortal hall consolidates class facilities and cannot provide independent Barracks/Range/Stable progression. Siege workshop, wall/watchtower, castle and some civic roles are absent or consolidated. `building-reference.csv` records26 published categories ×25 levels, with four ordinary resource costs and base seconds;650 rows. Published requirements, special materials, capacities and modifier applicability require per-category version/mode verification; costs alone are not implementation-ready specifications. One accessible secondary extraction does not certify current live values.

## C — Troops

## E — Combat mathematics

Aggregate base damage is count×attack×.05; incoming defense normalization is120/max(1,defense). These are original equations. Class counters use a bounded mixed-composition ±15% expression. Skills use local rage/cadence/factor/shield/heal rules. Secondary passives are missing from the key modifier path and secondary level/gear add unsupported scaling. Full AoE, silence/debuff, multi-target and reference damage-type interactions are not implemented. Hidden RoK damage and casualty equations remain UNKNOWN; require observed controlled combats, not a copied displayed power value.

## F — Commanders

The newly recorded secondary-active source conflict must be resolved before any exact timing claim. Both primary and secondary equipment applicability need explicit versioned evidence.

## G — Research

Full prerequisite/rank/bonus/cost/time/Academy research is required before a25-level city rebuild or T5 parity claim.

## H — Resources/economy

Food/wood/stone/gold/Mana are ordinary; Aether is premium. Production per five seconds is2×buildingLevel×(1+.15×harvest), except Mana's base1. Fractional carry prevents rounding losses. Passive cap=(500+250×vault+50×Citadel)×(1+.08×stores)×1000 under profile1. Gathering/loot can exceed that soft production cap. Cargo uses typed troop loads and original logistics/caravan multipliers. Transport tax is ceil(10%×sent); daily limit3000. A full source-referenced economy model must add missing VIP/territory/resource-specific effects and reference node levels. The simulation demonstrates local affordability; it is not a measured RoK economic trajectory.

## I — Hospitals/casualties

Local capacity=(25+15×infirmary)×50. Treatment weight=sum(count×tier); food2×weight, gold1×weight; time=max(2,1.2×weight/treatmentSpeed), plus mythic terms. These are original formulas, not reference tier prices. Local retreat and hospital admission prevent certain lethal losses, unlike complete mode-specific overflow/death rules. Typed conservation and replay/restart checks are valuable functional evidence but cannot establish reference casualty ratios. Four hospitals, per-building progression and battle-type distinctions require a joint design.

## J — Ordinary PvE
