# Numerical reference and evidence

Checked 2026-11-14. Machine-readable facts and per-column provenance are in `../server/balance-reference.json`; ordinary levels 1–25 and forts 1–5 are complete there. These are public fan-maintained tables, not official combat equations. GachaData was read live. Fandom direct requests failed (robots/access restriction); its publicly indexed text was read, reported crawled four months earlier. No account, paid API or workbook publication was used.

[Enemy power, base XP and AP](https://gacha-data.com/rise-of-kingdoms/barbarians/) and [troop attributes](https://gacha-data.com/rise-of-kingdoms/troops/) are documented values, medium confidence. Enemy power is neither troop power nor an attack requirement. Reject the duplicate T1 Spearman row with zero attack; civilization-specific T5 variants differ.

[Recommendations, lineups and advertised rewards](https://riseofkingdoms.fandom.com/wiki/Barbarians) give recommended armies, not guaranteed wins. Early XP is 106 times level versus GachaData's 100 times level; a 6% bonus is a plausible inference, not established causation. Use GachaData base XP. Missing NPC counts remain null. The level-25 lineup is mislabeled level22 and is uncertain. The advertised level-25 speedup count is four, despite five at level24; retain the discrepancy. Advertised food and wood are alternatives in a resource pool. Gem and rare-material probabilities are unknown; advertised gems must not become guaranteed currency. Tomes, speedups and one resource branch are described as guaranteed; the fourth rare item is optional. First-clear quests are separate from ordinary loot; incomplete quest amounts must not be invented.

[Capacity](https://riseofkingdoms.fandom.com/wiki/Troop_Capacity) adds City Hall and primary commander capacity, with percentage modifiers. Missing commander rows require explicitly marked interpolation. Five ToM Citadel ages cannot reproduce all 25 City Hall progression steps. [Commander experience](https://riseofkingdoms.fandom.com/wiki/Commanders/Experience) varies by rarity; row labels and the prose about next-level costs require careful cumulative interpretation. Advanced level30 contains a typo, levels49–50 depart from the usual ratio and levels51+ are missing. Do not silently fabricate an exact complete curve.

[Forts](https://riseofkingdoms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort) require rallies and have separate contribution-based loot. The quoted approximate 50% book chance is a community estimate, not an exact probability. GachaData separately lists fort AP150 and XP; ordinary AP50 and ordinary rewards do not apply to forts.

The requested 1500 AP cap and 36-second regeneration are adopted as the user's baseline, with secondary corroboration noted below; no official primary evidence established. The indexed barbarian article documents chain reductions of two and a peacekeeping reduction up to ten, but does not establish the chain floor. Discounts remain disabled pending a verified bounded specification. No free splash chaining or premium purchasing is introduced.

Hidden damage equations, patrol radii, spawn cadence, exact drop probabilities, hospital overflow and commander/building/research power have not been independently established. Any ToM implementation of these must be labeled provisional and measured through the engine. Passing table assertions does not establish combat parity.

Troop provenance is recorded in the JSON. Cavalry T3 comparison163/158 conflicts with its detailed card158/163: use the detailed card with reduced confidence. Cavalry T2–T4 food/wood splits are inferred from T1 proportions pending corroboration. T5 cavalry food800/stone600/gold400 follows GachaData, rather than extrapolating lower-tier costs. Generic infantry T5 attributes are corroborated by [Infantry](https://riseofkingdoms.fandom.com/wiki/Troops/Infantry); cavalry cards by [Cavalry](https://riseofkingdoms.fandom.com/wiki/Troops/Cavalry). Costs inferred in this paragraph are not verified parity.

An additional community [AP guide](https://prokingdoms.com/blog/mastering-barbarian-chaining-in-rise-of-kingdoms-the-ultimate-ap-efficiency-guide), checked2026-11-14, corroborates1500 maximum,100 per hour and36 seconds per point. This is independent secondary corroboration, not official documentation. Other older guides report1000/60 seconds or boosted eight-hour refills; those are not silently mixed into the baseline.
