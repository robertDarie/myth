Frontier Captain presentation mesh

Source: Meshy generation "Solar Centurion", archive Meshy_AI_Solar_Centurion_20261111_texture_fbx.zip, supplied 2026-11-11.
Measured source: 877,267 vertices, 1,754,682 triangles, one UV map, one material, base color, normal, metallic and roughness at 2048. No skeleton and no animation.
This derivative: 23,926 vertices, 48,000 triangles, the same UV map, a 22-bone generic skeleton (the original 21 plus a non-deforming SpearGrip on the right hand), and four skeletal clips (Idle, Ready, Attack, Victory).

Suncrest Aegis, archive Meshy_AI_Suncrest_Aegis_20261111_texture_fbx.zip. Source 481,367 vertices, 962,878 triangles. Presentation mesh 5,928 vertices, 12,000 triangles, same UV map, parented to LeftLowerArm. Packed map SuncrestAegis_MetallicSmoothness.png.
Sunforged Spear, archive Meshy_AI_Sunforged_Spear_20261111_texture_fbx.zip. Source 31,414 vertices, 62,828 triangles. Presentation mesh 4,000 vertices, 8,000 triangles, same UV map, parented to SpearGrip. Packed map SunforgedSpear_MetallicSmoothness.png.
Equipped presentation total: 68,000 triangles. The right-hand fingertips were closed onto the shaft in the bind pose, and the four clips were rebuilt around that grip. The source zips and the unreduced FBX files stay in work/asset-inbox/solar-centurion/ and are not part of this commit.
Metallic and roughness were packed into FrontierCaptain_MetallicSmoothness.png (metallic in RGB, smoothness in alpha) for Unity's built-in Standard shader.
