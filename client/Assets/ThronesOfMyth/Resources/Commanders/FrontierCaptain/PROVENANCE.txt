Frontier Captain presentation mesh

Source: Meshy generation "Solar Centurion", archive Meshy_AI_Solar_Centurion_20261111_texture_fbx.zip, supplied 2026-11-11.
Measured source: 877,267 vertices, 1,754,682 triangles, one UV map, one material, base color, normal, metallic and roughness at 2048. No skeleton and no animation.
This derivative: 23,926 vertices, 48,000 triangles, the same UV map, a 21-bone humanoid skeleton, and four skeletal clips (Idle, Ready, Attack, Victory).
Metallic and roughness were packed into FrontierCaptain_MetallicSmoothness.png (metallic in RGB, smoothness in alpha) for Unity's built-in Standard shader.
